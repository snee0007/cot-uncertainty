CoT Uncertainty Visualiser

// step-level hallucination detection via multi-trace consistency analysis

01
Sample 3 traces
Sends your question to the LLM 3 times independently to get different chain-of-thought responses.
02
Cross-trace analysis
Compares reasoning steps across all 3 traces. Steps that differ or contradict each other signal higher uncertainty.
03
Score & visualise
Each reasoning step gets an uncertainty score 0–1 with an explanation, colour-coded for fast scanning.
